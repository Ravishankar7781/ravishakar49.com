Projects finished during Zero to Data Analyst. Every entry links to the learner's own work, so recruiters can see it too.

Send yours in

How to submit

Finished a week's challenge or project? DM SHOWCASE on Instagram with these four things:

  1. Your name as you want it shown.
  2. A public link to your work: GitHub, Tableau Public, a Kaggle notebook, or a Google Drive file set to "anyone with the link".
  3. One sentence on the most interesting thing you found.
  4. Your LinkedIn (optional), if you want recruiters to find you.
DM SHOWCASE on InstagramUse the form instead

What gets featured

  • Your own work, built along with a week of the series.
  • A clear question, an answer and one chart or table that shows it.
  • Code or files anyone can open. AI help is fine; say where you used it.
  • Sending it in means you're happy for your name, link and sentence to appear here. Ask any time to have it removed.

I review every entry before it goes up, usually within a week.
